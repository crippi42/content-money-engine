Step 1: Choose Your Website Purpose

1

Decide what your website will be:

  • Personal blog or portfolio
  • Small business site
  • eCommerce store (WooCommerce/Shopify)
  • Agency/client site

Your purpose determines which plan and features you need:

  • Blog/portfolio: Premium plan is sufficient
  • Business site with email: Cloud Startup plan recommended (free domain, email)
  • eCommerce: Cloud Startup plan (SSL, daily backups)
  • Multiple clients: Unlimited or Cloud Startup plan

Step 2: Choose Your Domain

2

Select a domain name. Options:

  • Register a new domain through Hostinger (free for 1 year with Cloud Startup plan)
  • Transfer an existing domain to Hostinger (transfer fee applies)
  • Use a subdomain (e.g., yourname.hostingerapp.com) — free but less professional

Domain tips:

  • Keep it short and easy to spell
  • Use .com if possible (most trusted)
  • Avoid numbers and hyphens
  • Check social media handle availability early

Step 3: Select a Hosting Plan

3

Choose your plan based on your purpose (see table above). Consider:

  • Term length: Longer terms (48 months) have the lowest per-month cost
  • Renewal cost: Prices increase at renewal — budget accordingly
  • Features: Only pay for what you need initially
  • Migration: If moving from another host, check if they offer free migration
Important: Hostinger offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. You can test the service and cancel if it doesn't meet your needs.

Step 4: Complete Hosting Setup

4

After selecting your plan:

  1. Enter your domain choice (new, transfer, or skip)
  2. Fill in account details
  3. Review and complete payment
  4. Check your email for login credentials and setup instructions

You will receive an email with your hosting account details and a link to the Hostinger hPanel (their custom control panel).

Step 5: Connect/Configure Your Domain

5

If you registered a new domain through Hostinger, it is automatically connected. If you transferred a domain:

  1. Point your domain's nameservers to Hostinger: ns1.dns-parking.com and ns2.dns-parking.com
  2. Allow 24-48 hours for DNS propagation (usually faster with Hostinger's DNS)

You can check DNS status in the hPanel under "DNS Zone Editor."

Step 6: Install WordPress (or Site Software)

6

Hostinger offers one-click WordPress installation:

  1. Log into your Hostinger hPanel
  2. Navigate to Auto Installer
  3. Select your domain and click "Install" next to WordPress
  4. Fill in your site name, admin username, and password
  5. Click "Install"

Alternative options:

  • Use Hostinger Website Builder (drag-and-drop, no coding)
  • Install another CMS (Drupal, Joomla) via the same auto-installer
  • Start with a blank site for custom development

Estimated time: Installation completes in under 2 minutes.

Step 7: Configure Essential Settings

7

After WordPress installation:

  1. Update permalinks: Go to Settings → Permalinks → select "Post name"
  2. Install SSL: SSL is included — verify the lock icon appears
  3. Choose a lightweight theme: Astra, GeneratePress, or Hostinger's free themes
  4. Install essential plugins:
    • Rank Math or Yoast SEO (SEO)
    • WP Super Cache or LiteSpeed Cache (performance)
    • UpdraftPlus (backups — Hostinger includes this on Cloud Startup plan)
    • Wordfence (security)
  5. Set timezone and region: Settings → General
Efficiency tip: Hostinger's Cloud Startup plan includes free domain, daily backups, and email accounts, which eliminates several paid add-ons you would otherwise need to purchase separately.

Step 8: Launch and Verify

8

Before going live:

  1. Write and publish your first post or page
  2. Test all links and forms
  3. Verify the site loads on mobile and desktop
  4. Use SSL checker tools to confirm HTTPS
  5. Submit your site to Google Search Console for indexing

After launch:

  • Monitor uptime and performance
  • Schedule regular backups
  • Set up analytics (Google Analytics free, or Plausible for privacy-focused)

Yes. Hostinger allows plan upgrades at any time. The cost difference is prorated.
