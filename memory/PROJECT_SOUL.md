# Content Money Engine — Project Soul

## Core Purpose
Build an automated, multi-agent system that discovers profitable content opportunities, creates content, distributes it, tracks revenue, and learns to improve future decisions — all operating within the Multi-AI Workstation's §26 Human Approval Gate.

## Business Hypothesis
A systematic, repeatable pipeline that finds underserved buyer-intent content opportunities and produces targeted assets can generate sustainable affiliate revenue while remaining approval-gated for all consequential actions.

## Non-Goals (Initially)
- Building an empire of 100 sites
- Manual one-off publishing
- Any publishing without human approval
- Real-time trading or high-frequency operations

## Success Definition (MVP)
- 1 niche identified and documented
- 3 ranked opportunities with score ≥ threshold
- 1 piece of content created, reviewed, and approved for publishing
- 1 published via 1 channel
- Measurable traffic/click data returned within 30 days
- Analyst produces feedback report feeding back to Researcher
