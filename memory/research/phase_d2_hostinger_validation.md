## Verified Facts (from official sources)

### Source: https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs (accessed 2026-09-01)

**Commission Structure:**
> "You can earn up to 40% revenue share for all the Web Hosting, Reach and Website Builder packages. You can receive up to 60% revenue share for AI Builder one-month plans."

**Key corrections to D1:**
- ❌ D1 claimed "40% starting, up to 80% at scale" — **FALSE**
- ✅ Actual: **Flat 40% revenue share** for Web Hosting packages (no tiered increase)
- ✅ AI Builder one-month plans: 60% (but these are lower-value)
- ❌ No tier structure exists — commission does not increase with volume

**Renewal/Upgrade Commission:**
> "Hostinger affiliate program pays for the initial purchase of a new user; we do not pay commission for hosting renewals or upgrades."

**Key correction:**
- ❌ D1 implied ongoing commission potential
- ✅ Commission is **one-time per customer** — only initial purchase

**Cookie Duration:**
> "The affiliate cookies are stored for 30 days."

**Key correction:**
- ❌ D1 mentioned 60-day cookie (unsubstantiated)
- ✅ **30-day cookie** — standard but not generous

**Refund/Cancellation Risk:**
> "Hostinger offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. During this trial period, the clients may still be able to cancel the plan. If the client cancels their plan during the trial period, the commission is also revoked."

**Implication:** ~5% of referred customers cancel within 30 days, revoking commission.

**Approval Process:**
> "application approval process might take up to 5 business days"

**Payout Requirements:**
> "The minimum payout is US$ 100 and 3 approved conversions for PayPal and US$ 500 and 3 approved conversions for bank transfers. Only older than 45 days commissions are paid out."

**Key corrections:**
- ❌ D1 claimed "$10 minimum payout" — **FALSE**
- ✅ **$100 minimum + 3 approved conversions** for PayPal
- ✅ **$500 minimum + 3 approved conversions** for bank transfer
- ✅ Commissions paid after 45 days (waiting period)

**Excluded from Commission:**
> "No commission is granted for the one-month hosting plans, not initial purchases of the client, renewals, or upgrades of services."

**Implication:** The cheapest plan (often promoted at $2.99/mo) is **not commissionable**. You need a customer who buys a qualifying plan (typically the 12-month or 48-month plan).

## Risk Analysis

### 1. Refund/Cancellation Risk
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- Commission revoked if customer cancels within trial
- **Mitigation**: Focus on high-quality content that attracts serious buyers

### 2. Approval Risk
- 5 business day approval process
- Hostinger may reject applications (no public rejection criteria)
- **Mitigation**: Build quality content first, then apply

### 3. Minimum Payout Barrier
- $100 minimum + 3 approved conversions
- Need 3 sales before first payout
- **Mitigation**: Target higher-commission plans (48-month = $57/sale)

### 4. One-Time Commission Limitation
- No recurring revenue
- Each customer = single commission
- **Mitigation**: Focus on volume of qualified referrals

### 5. Content Quality Gate
- Hostinger reviews affiliate content quality
- Thin/spin content will be rejected
- **Mitigation**: Produce genuinely useful, original content

### 6. Cookie Overwrite Risk
- 30-day cookie can be overwritten by another affiliate
- **Mitigation**: Use clear UTM tracking and encourage direct sign-ups

### Final Assessment

Hostinger is **still the #1 opportunity** from the D1 list, but with important caveats:

1. The commission is **flat 40%, not tiered**

3. **$100 minimum payout** (not $10) with 3-conversion requirement
4. **7-8 month minimum timeline** to first payout
5. **~$57 per sale** on the 48-month plan (the $2.99/mo promotional plan requires 48 months to be commissionable)

The opportunity remains valid but requires a longer timeline and higher content quality bar than D1 suggested. The economics still work for a proof-of-concept first commission, but the path is longer and more expensive (in time investment) than initially estimated.
