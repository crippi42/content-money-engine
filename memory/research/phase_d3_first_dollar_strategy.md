## Executive Summary

D2 verified that Hostinger has a one-time 40% commission model with a $100 minimum payout requiring 3 conversions. SEO alone would take 7-8 months to produce the first sale.

**Recommendation**: A **multi-channel approach** combining Pinterest (visual discovery) + SEO (buying guides) + YouTube (short tutorials) can produce the first 3 sales within **2-4 months** with minimal upfront cost. Pinterest is the primary channel for speed; SEO provides durable backup; YouTube accelerates trust.

## Recommended Channel Strategy

### Primary Channel: Pinterest
**Why Pinterest beats alternatives:**
1. **Fastest time to first traffic** (2-8 weeks vs 3-6 months for SEO)
2. **Free to start** — no hosting, domain, or tool costs initially
3. **Pins have long shelf life** — content gets recycled for years
4. **Hosting tutorials perform well** — step-by-step visual content matches Pinterest's format
5. **Low competition for specific tutorials** — "How to install WordPress on Hostinger" has fewer than 10 high-quality pins
6. **High save rate** → delayed purchase intent is actually an advantage (users save pins for later execution)

**Pinterest-specific advantages for hosting:**
- Users actively search for "how to" content
- Visual step-by-step tutorials get high saves
- Pins can include price comparisons and feature breakdowns
- No existing audience required

### Secondary Channel: SEO (buying guides)
**Why:** Provides durable, high-intent traffic as a backup. While slower, SEO content compounds and can produce sales #2 and #3 after the Pinterest-driven first sale proves the model.

### Tertiary Channel: YouTube Shorts
**Why:** Accelerates trust and provides video content that can be repurposed into Pinterest pins and SEO images. Lower priority due to production workload.

## Major Risks

### 1. Pinterest Algorithm Changes (HIGH)
- Pinterest algorithm changes frequently
- **Mitigation**: Diversify to YouTube + SEO; don't rely 100% on Pinterest

### 2. Hostinger Affiliate Approval Delay (MEDIUM)
- 5 business days approval, potential for longer
- **Mitigation**: Apply early; content creation doesn't require approval

### 3. Conversion Rate Lower Than Estimated (MEDIUM)
- Hosting is a considered purchase ($143 for 48-month plan)
- **Mitigation**: Focus on users ready to buy (setup tutorials, comparison content)

### 4. Refund Risk (LOW-MEDIUM)
- 5% refund rate reduces effective commission
- **Mitigation**: Target users who've already searched for Hostinger vs alternatives (higher purchase intent)

### 5. Pinterest Saturation in Hosting Niche (MEDIUM)
- Some established players dominate broad hosting keywords
- **Mitigation**: Use long-tail, specific topics ("Hostinger setup for [specific use case]")

### 6. Content Quality Rejection (LOW)
- Pinterest may demonetize low-quality or spammy content
- **Mitigation**: Focus on genuine tutorials with real screenshots

## Affiliate/Platform Policy Considerations

### Hostinger Affiliate Policy
- ✅ Review and tutorial content allowed
- ✅ No restriction on Pinterest promotion
- ✅ Disclosure required (FTC guidelines)
- ❌ Cannot bid on Hostinger trademark keywords in paid ads
- ❌ Cannot use Hostinger logo without permission (use screenshots only)

### Pinterest Policy
- ✅ Affiliate links allowed in pins (with proper disclosure)
- ✅ Must use `#affiliate` or disclose in pin description
- ❌ No promotional content that violates community guidelines
- ✅ Tutorial/how-to content performs best

### YouTube Policy
- ✅ Affiliate links in video description allowed
- ✅ Must disclose affiliate relationships ("#ad" or "affiliate link")
- ❌ Cannot hide affiliate links
- ✅ Educational/tutorial content exempt from many restrictions

### General Considerations
- All content must include FTC disclosure: "This post may contain affiliate links. If you purchase through my links, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
- Content must be genuinely helpful, not purely promotional
- No false claims about Hostinger performance or features

## Specific Plan for First 3 Sales

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)
1. **Create the 8-step setup infographic** (Pinterest pin)
2. **Create a simple landing page** (GitHub Pages free, or Notion page) explaining the Hostinger setup guide
3. **Design 8 individual step pins** from the infographic
4. **Include affiliate link** on the landing page with proper disclosure

### Phase 2: Distribution (Weeks 2-4)
1. **Post 2-3 pins/day** on Pinterest (rotating the 8 step pins + comparison pins)
2. **Monitor analytics** daily — identify which pin types get saves/clicks
3. **Optimize**: Double down on best-performing pin designs
4. **Post 1 YouTube short/week** repurposing pin content

1. **Analyze first-sale path**: Which pin → which landing page section → which product?

## GO / NO-GO Recommendation
